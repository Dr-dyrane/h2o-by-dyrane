## Submission title

H₂O by Dyrane — A body of work in motion

## Public URL

## One-line description

A doctor, product designer and software engineer turns fifty real builds into one living current of systems, consequences and accumulated judgment.

## Short submission description

H₂O is Alexander Udeogaranya’s spatial portfolio: medicine, product design, software engineering and operations treated as one continuous practice. A scroll-linked liquid world connects six live systems while semantic HTML, reduced motion and adaptive WebGL keep the experience usable beyond the spectacle.

## Submission copy

### Design

One borderless visual law governs the experience: depth, light, scale, typography and movement replace cards and chrome. Each project receives an authored crop, one memorable idea, one proof line and one clear exit.

### Creativity

The WebGL current is generated as a persistent spatial metaphor for how patterns and judgment move between projects. It is not a detached hero object; scroll, project state and color continually reshape the same world. MyFinance extends the same philosophy into product communication: its discovery route renders the real responsive product rather than describing it through a conventional stack of marketing sections.

### Usability

Essential content remains semantic HTML. Project scenes are direct links, focus states remain visible, reduced motion has complete parity, constrained devices receive a compact renderer and the experience survives WebGL failure.

### Content

The six flagships are real products rather than speculative exercises. iVisit is live as a patient experience and on Google Play; MyFinance opens directly into its public `/discover` product story; the remaining chapters link to their public products.
